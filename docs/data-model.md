# Expense register

AccountContext is an agent-operated expense register, not a general ledger or tax filing system. It launches empty. Supplier invoices, receipts and credit notes are distinct from payments and subscriptions. Founder expense claims are distinct from company bills. Recording reimbursement never executes a transfer. Manual files and CSV import are the initial ingestion paths; there are no vendor API connections or outgoing messages.

See [schema fields](schema-fields.json) and [the exported SQL configuration](../pocketcontext.json) for literal names. Parties represent suppliers and billed entities; billing accounts associate a supplier and billed party. Unknown legal details remain blank. Bills retain source document, service dates, net/tax/gross, currency, purpose and category. Optional bill lines must reconcile net, tax and gross before review. Subscription amounts describe future commitments, not incurred expenses.

Bills move from draft to reviewed to void. Reviewed financial evidence cannot be edited or returned to draft. Void existing allocations before voiding a settled bill, and record a new bill referencing `correction_of`. Credit notes must reference a reviewed original bill with matching supplier/currency; their cumulative nonvoid amount cannot exceed that bill. Refund payments use direction `refund`, and allocate only to credit notes. Refund totals and outgoing payments remain separate measures. Credit notes are refund receivables in v1; there is no automatic offset against unpaid invoices. Reports must present payable and refund receivable balances separately rather than silently netting settlement status.

Payments have positive amounts, an explicit payer when known, optional source document, and separately identified fee_minor. The payment amount is the amount available for expense allocation; fees are additional and are never silently included in allocated totals. Payments and allocations are immutable except for one-way voiding with a reason. Void allocations before voiding a payment. Payment allocation capacity is shared between bills and claims, so one transfer cannot be counted twice. Limits and writes run inside a single writer transaction, including batches.

Claims start as drafts owned by the submitting user. Submission requires a document owned by that claimant, business purpose and payer. Submitted evidence and amounts are immutable. Submitted claims can be approved, rejected or voided; approved or rejected claims can only be voided. Approval/rejection requires `can_approve`, separately from finance access. Alberto's approved policy permits self-approval; the application has no prohibition on approvers approving their own claims. A void/rejection needs a decision note. Reimbursements require approved claims and cannot exceed claim or payment balances. Void reimbursements before removing approval.

## Money and conversion

Every monetary value is an integer between zero and 9,007,199,254,740,991; allocation and payment amounts must be positive. Supported currency/exponent pairs are USD/EUR/GBP/CHF/CAD/AUD/INR = 2, JPY = 0 and KWD = 3. Other currencies require an explicit implementation/policy update. Clients must submit currency_exponent; the numeric default zero only works for zero-exponent currencies.

Allocations retain both invoice/claim-currency and payment-currency minor amounts. Same-currency amounts/exponents must match. Cross-currency allocations require a positive rate, date and source. The rate means payment major units per invoice/claim major unit. The rounded product may differ from the supplied payment amount by at most one payment minor unit for documented settlement rounding. This is allocation validation, not a general-purpose decimal valuation engine. The supplied integer allocation amounts are authoritative. Never produce a converted financial report without reviewed exchange-rate evidence. Default reports group by currency.

## Identities, reads and evidence

The existing PocketBase `users` collection serves humans and agents. Verified Google Workspace JIT preserves ordinary own-claim access only; initial finance assignments are operator provisioning, never first-login privileges. Operator-only `finance_members(account,can_approve)` is source-only and excluded from SQL. Removing membership immediately affects subsequent writes, snapshots and protected downloads; snapshots already in flight may finish. Disabled-user handling and seven-day tokens reuse RaiseContext's tested account lifecycle.

Finance members see all exported financial tables. Ordinary users see only their own claims, documents, reimbursement allocations and associated audit history; safe user-directory access is self-only except finance. All ordinary REST list/view routes are locked, including relation expansion and realtime records. Protected document downloads are the exception: the `protectedFile` view context allows owner or finance, with PocketBase file tokens. Possession of a filename is insufficient. Successful authorized writes return their record payload; no foreign records are expanded.

Documents contain one protected original file (maximum 20 MiB; PDF, PNG, JPEG, text or CSV), owner and title. Upload computes SHA-256 from stored bytes server-side before transaction completion. All document metadata and originals are immutable after creation, including operator REST. Correction means creating a replacement document. The container requires `sha256sum`. Originals are stored locally under PocketBase storage and included in independently verified full backups; database replication alone is insufficient.

All business writes have server-managed attribution and revision. Updates require expected_revision and return conflict on stale values. Audit rows are written transactionally, retain before/after changed fields, actor and claimant owner, and cannot be edited or deleted through REST. Reimbursement audit ownership follows the claim; payment records remain finance-only. Original file bodies are not copied into audit. Business record deletion is forbidden. Superuser maintenance remains a privileged recovery boundary and must not be used for ordinary expense operations.

Import batches have unique SHA-256 values; the portable CSV workflow writes deterministic records and its batch marker atomically. Bill numbers are unique per supplier/kind among nonvoid bills. Export batches record finance-authorized export status and manifest checksum; exports include source CSVs and original bytes/checksums. Export delivery to an accountant requires a separate instruction.

## Provenance and validation

Identity lifecycle and transactional audit structure were adapted from RaiseContext `44f8f10537388f9a934a2bc1800d3df6a046c580`; filtered snapshots follow PeopleContext `b41a0f26f674222ae24b9268029a65c867ffaa99`. Domain schema and financial invariants are AccountContext-owned. The server pin is recorded in POCKETCONTEXT_VERSION. Tests use synthetic data and isolated databases, including concurrent writes, partial USD/EUR allocation, refunds, one-minor-unit rounding, protected file bytes, audit rollback, visibility and role revocation.
